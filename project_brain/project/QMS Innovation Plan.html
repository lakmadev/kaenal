The Core Shift
Reactive → Proactive Quality
The industry calls this "Shift-Left Quality" — embedding quality enforcement earlier in the value chain, before defects become costly NCRs.
Current State — Reactive
Problem occurs → System responds
NCR raised after defect found in production
8D triggered after customer complaint arrives
CAPA opened after audit failure is recorded
Supplier risk assessed after delivery failure
FMEA reviewed only at design freeze
The Shift
Future State — Proactive
System predicts → Team prevents
AI flags risk patterns during design review stage
Digital twins simulate failure modes before build
IoT anomalies detected before process drifts out of spec
Supplier intelligence forecasts delivery quality in advance
Live FMEA scoring evolves as design decisions are made
💡
The Business Case
The cost of a defect found during design is ~1×. The same defect found during production is ~10×. At the customer is ~100×. KAENAL's reactive system already captures the 10× and 100× events well — this plan targets the 1× opportunity that competitors have not yet productized.
Innovation Pillars
7 Methods to Introduce
Each pillar is a distinct capability module that can be built independently and activated progressively.
Pillar 01 — AI Layer
Predictive Quality Intelligence Engine
Train ML models on KAENAL's historical NCR, CAPA, FMEA, and inspection data to score the predicted defect probability of new designs and processes before they enter production. The engine surfaces a "Quality Risk Score" at every design gate, continuously learning from each new outcome.
ML scoring model Design gate integration Confidence bands Continuous learning
Pillar 02 — Digital Twin
Process & Product Digital Twins
Create virtual replicas of manufacturing processes and product assemblies. Engineers run quality simulations — tolerance stacks, thermal cycles, vibration profiles — in the twin before physical prototyping. Failure modes are predicted, ranked by RPN, and linked directly into KAENAL's FMEA workbench.
Virtual simulation FMEA linkage What-if scenarios BOM integration
Pillar 03 — Shift-Left
Development-Stage Quality Gates
Embed quality checkpoints directly inside the engineering workflow — at concept, design review, DV testing, PV testing, and PPAP. Each gate auto-scores risk using live FMEA RPN, open NCR patterns, and supplier history. Gates block progression when thresholds are breached, with an AI-assisted override rationale flow.
5 gate types Auto-RPN scoring ECN integration Override audit trail
Pillar 04 — Real-Time Sensing
IoT Process Anomaly Detection
Integrate with shop-floor IoT sensors (temperature, torque, vibration, pressure, cycle time) to stream real-time process data into KAENAL's SPC engine. An ML anomaly detector identifies early drift signals — before the process crosses control limits — and triggers an alert, not just a record. Operators get a prescriptive action, not just a flag.
OPC-UA / MQTT SPC enhancement Drift alerts Prescriptive actions
Pillar 05 — Vision AI
Inline Computer Vision Inspection
Deploy AI-powered visual inspection at strategic production checkpoints via camera integration. The model is trained on accepted/rejected sample images from existing inspections. Defects are classified by type and severity in real time, auto-creating a draft NCR with photographic evidence and defect coordinates — zero manual logging.
Camera integration Auto-NCR creation Defect classification Edge-deployable
Pillar 06 — Supply Chain
Proactive Supplier Quality Forecasting
Extend the supplier scorecard with a forward-looking risk model: combine PPM trends, delivery history, open SCARs, capacity utilisation signals, and geopolitical data to forecast quality risk 4–8 weeks out. Automated early-warning triggers allow procurement to intervene before a bad batch ships — not after it arrives.
Risk forecasting 4–8 week horizon Capacity signals Auto-SCAR trigger
Pillar 07 — Closed Loop
Continuous Learning & Knowledge Amplification
Every post-production problem KAENAL captures feeds back into the predictive models for the next development cycle. Root causes from 8D cases, CAPA outcomes, FMEA updates, and escaped defects are structured into a quality knowledge graph — so the system gets smarter with every product generation. This transforms KAENAL from a record-keeping tool into an institutional memory engine.
Closed-Loop Cycle
1
Capture — every NCR, 8D, CAPA, audit finding enters structured data
2
Classify — AI categorises root cause, product family, process type
3
Train — outcome updates predictive model weights quarterly
4
Predict — refined risk scores surface on next design cycle
5
Prevent — action taken in design; the loop closes
Knowledge graph Auto-retraining pipeline Cross-product learning Root cause taxonomy Horizontal deployment
Implementation Roadmap
4 Phases · 18 Months
Sequenced to deliver early wins while building toward full predictive capability. Each phase ships usable features — no big bang.
Q1
Phase 1 — Q3 2026 · Months 1–4
Data Foundation & Quick Wins
Months 1–4
Pillar 07 (partial): Structure and tag all historical NCR, CAPA, 8D data for ML training readiness. Build quality knowledge taxonomy.
Pillar 03: Introduce Development-Stage Quality Gates within the existing ECN and PPAP modules — 3 gates initially (Design Review, DV Test, PPAP).
Pillar 04 (sensing): Ship IoT data ingestion connectors (OPC-UA/MQTT) and plumb live data into enhanced SPC charts. Alert on control-limit approach, not just breach.
Establish AI model governance policies in existing AI Governance Hub for predictive features.
Q2
Phase 2 — Q4 2026 · Months 5–9
Predictive Intelligence & Supplier Foresight
Months 5–9
Pillar 01: Train and deploy the Predictive Quality Engine (v1). Integrate Quality Risk Score into the Dashboard KPI panel and each Design Gate.
Pillar 06: Launch Supplier Quality Forecasting. Surface 4-week forward risk scores on Supplier detail pages and add an "At-Risk Suppliers" widget to the Dashboard.
Pillar 04 (anomaly detection): Layer ML anomaly detection on IoT streams. Move from threshold alerts to predictive drift warnings.
Extend the Knowledge Graph Explorer to visualise predictive signal chains — "this design decision correlates with NCR-type X".
Q3
Phase 3 — Q1–Q2 2027 · Months 10–14
Digital Twins & Vision Inspection
Months 10–14
Pillar 02: Launch Process Digital Twin module. Integrate with CAD/PLM systems via API. Output: simulated failure mode list → auto-populates FMEA workbench draft.
Pillar 05: Roll out Computer Vision Inspection at pilot plants. Camera SDK + edge model deployment. Auto-NCR creation with image evidence and defect coordinates.
Pillar 03 (full): Expand quality gates to all 5 types. Activate AI-assisted override rationale flow with approval chain.
Mobile Inspector app gains real-time risk score display for items under inspection.
Q4
Phase 4 — Q3 2027 · Months 15–18
Closed Loop & Full System Intelligence
Months 15–18
Pillar 07 (full): Auto-retraining pipeline live. Every closed NCR/8D/CAPA automatically enriches the predictive model. Quarterly model validation against held-out test sets.
Pillar 01 (v2): Predictive engine upgraded with cross-product and cross-tenant (anonymised) learning. Benchmark model against industry defect rates.
New Proactive Quality Dashboard — a dedicated cockpit showing predicted vs. actual quality, gate pass rates, twin simulation vs. real-world accuracy, and AI model performance.
Open Prediction API in Developer Platform — allow external tools (PLM, ERP, CAD) to pull quality risk scores programmatically.
