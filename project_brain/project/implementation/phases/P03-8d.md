**Status:** Backend ✅ done · FE 🟡 partial
**Design jsx:** `eightd.jsx`, `eightd-agentic.jsx`, `eightd-templates.jsx`, `eightd-pdf.jsx`

## 1. Feature scope (from jsx)
- **List** (Active / Completed) with D1–D8 mini progress stepper, team lead, status, dates.
- **Detail** — stepped workflow + step navigator:
  - **D1** Team · **D2** Problem (statement, **IS / IS-NOT** table, quantification, customer impact) · **D3** Interim Containment (+ customer notification, effectiveness) · **D4** Root Cause (5 Whys, Fishbone, Fault tree, **AI suggestions w/ confidence + accept/dismiss**, escape point, verification) · **D5** Permanent CA (+ decision matrix scoring, verification plan) · **D6** Implement & Validate (checklist, before/after, customer validation) · **D7** Prevent Recurrence (systemic, training, control-plan/FMEA updates, horizontal deploy, poka-yoke) · **D8** Close & Congratulate (recognition, lessons, final metrics/cost, sign-offs).
- **Agentic AI copilot** (`eightd-agentic.jsx`): per-field AI draft controls, AI card headers, **provenance strip**, **copilot rail**, **Generate Pack** modal — drafts with source/confidence attribution.
- **8D templates** (`eightd-templates.jsx`): list + editor (industry prompts, default roles, SLA per step).
- **8D PDF** (`eightd-pdf.jsx`): branded D1–D8 report with diagrams + signatures.

## 6. FE fidelity rebuild — plan & decisions (2026-08-03)

The first 8D FE pass was a stripped skeleton (a discipline rail + per-field textareas) that
diverged badly from `eightd.jsx` — the exact failure `docs/design-rules.md` (rule #9) now forbids.
This rebuild reproduces the jsx surface in full. Decisions taken so the build stays inside the
settled backend without inventing scope:

- **All structured discipline content persists in the existing `steps[dN].data` jsonb** — no schema
  change. Conventions (a convention, not a contract, per the freeform `data`):
  - `d1.data.teamRoles` (`{userId: "Team Lead"|"Production"|…}`), `d1.data.ai` (provenance
    `{model, draftedFrom, draftedAt}`).
  - `d2.data`: `problemStatement`, `isIsNot` (`{what/where/when/howMuch/who: {is,isNot}}`), `cost`, `quantity`.
  - `d3.data`: `actions` (`[{title, owner, status}]`), `effective`, `aiContainment`.
  - `d4.data`: `fiveWhys`, `analyses` (the RCA method builders' saved state), `aiSuggestions`
    (ranked root causes), `similarCases`, `acceptedIndex` (accepted root cause), `rootCause`.
  - Accept-root-cause / apply-containment / RCA edits all persist through the existing
    `POST /v1/eight-ds/:id/steps/:n` mutation → audited in-transaction, optimistic-concurrency safe.
- **Members directory (`GET /v1/members`) shipped as the enabler** — the jsx shows real names,
  roles and avatars everywhere (`userById(id).name`), but the web app only had `UserCell` →
  "You"/truncated id. New read-only endpoint: roster from RLS `memberships`, names from
  `control.users` (control pool), gated `ncr:view` so partners can't enumerate internal staff.
  This is cross-cutting: NCR/CAPA/Suppliers/Audits owners & leads all resolve through it.
- **AI copilot rail / provenance strip** are reproduced in full and **bound to persisted `data`**
  (seeded on the demo record by `seed-demo.ts`). The *deterministic* review controls (approve/edit
  provenance, accept a ranked cause, apply a proposed containment into D3) are real and persisted.
  The **generation** of those AI drafts is the deferred delta: today they are seeded/authored; the
  LLM that produces ranked causes + containment from the linked NCR is [P23](P23-ai-assistant.md)
  via the existing `/v1/ai/drafts` gateway. Generate-Pack reproduces the jsx exactly (client-side
  markdown assembly + download); the *branded PDF* remains [P24](P24-pdf-designer.md).
- **RCA method builders** (5-Whys, Fishbone/6M, Pareto, 5W2H, Timeline & Change, Free-form) are
  client-side interactive tools; their state saves into `d4.data.analyses` — no backend beyond the
  step mutation.
- **Still deferred (surfaced, not silently dropped):** LLM generation of copilot drafts (P23);
  branded D1–D8 PDF (P24); 8D templates editor (`eightd-templates.jsx`); the D5–D8 rich panels
  (decision matrix, sign-offs) beyond their structured `data` fields — tracked in Known issues.
